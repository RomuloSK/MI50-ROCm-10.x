# Build resume notes

This filename is retained as a short redirect for older local checklists.
Machine-specific build paths, logs and artifact hashes are intentionally not
stored in the public repository.

Use [`BUILD_RECOVERY.md`](BUILD_RECOVERY.md) for portable recovery steps and
the pinned source and patch lock files for reproducibility. After a build,
keep generated reports and artifacts under the ignored `out/`, `build/`,
`artifacts/` or `dist/` directories.
